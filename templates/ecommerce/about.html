Where it began

A karkhana in Ason Bazaar, three generations deep.

Rhythm ko Dukan opened its shutters in Ason Bazaar in 1998, the third jewellery counter my grandfather ran in that same lane. What started as a single furnace and a handful of trusted karigars is now a full workshop — but the gold is still melted, cast, and set by hand, the same way it was thirty years ago.

Every Naugedi, Tilhari and Sirbandi that leaves our counter carries a government hallmark and a promise: what you see weighed in front of you is exactly what you take home.

Milestones

Three decades, briefly

1998

Rhythm ko Dukan opens its first counter in Ason Bazaar with two karigars and one furnace.

2007

First hallmarking certification secured; bridal Sirbandi sets become the shop's signature.

2016

Workshop expands to a full karkhana with six goldsmiths and a dedicated bridal fitting room.

2026

Rhythm ko Dukan goes online, bringing the Ason Bazaar counter to all of Nepal.

Our craft

Cast, hammered, and hand-finished — nothing imported.

Every piece is designed and cast in-house. Our karigars still finish filigree work — the fine twisted-wire detailing on our Jhumka and Bulaki pieces — entirely by hand, a skill passed down within three families working the shop today.

  • ✦ In-house casting & polishing
  • ✦ Hand filigree on traditional pieces
  • ✦ Custom sizing for rings & bangles
  • ✦ Old-gold melt & redesign service
